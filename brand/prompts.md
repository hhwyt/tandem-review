# Tandem poster prompts (Nano Banana Pro, 3:4)

## v1-engraving

Poster for an open-source developer tool called "TANDEM", tagline "Human steers. Agent pedals. Review together." Core metaphor: a tandem bicycle. The FRONT rider is a human software engineer holding the handlebars and looking ahead (the human decides direction). The REAR rider is an AI agent pedaling powerfully (the agent does the heavy lifting). Both pedal in sync. Render the word TANDEM large and perfectly spelled, the tagline small and perfectly spelled. No other text, no logos of real companies. Style: classical copperplate engraving / woodcut like an illustration from an old book of myths, fine cross-hatching, cream paper, black ink with a single accent color violet (#8250df). The road under the bicycle is made of lines of source code; some lines highlighted green (added) and red (removed) like a diff. The agent rider is drawn as a figure made of luminous violet line-work and small code glyphs, slightly translucent. Epic, heroic, timeless mood, like Sisyphus but hopeful: they are climbing a long hill together. Serif display type.
